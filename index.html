Boss fights

Three things I built on my own time. All public, all measured, and each one comes with the weak spot I found in it.

Red Team Attack Simulator

A security testing harness. It runs 40 known attack techniques against an AI model to measure how well its defenses hold, and an independent judge decides what counts as a hit.

Weak spot One person's test list against a handful of targets. It's not a benchmark. Run it on your own models before you trust it on yours.

Repo Live dashboard The attack log

1,824 attacks 78 got through 4.3% hit rate

Every square is one real attack from the log. Bright ones got through. Point at or tap one.

Prompt Shield

Catches prompt injection in two tiers: instant rules first, then an LLM judge for anything the rules can't settle.

Attacks caught 16.2%about 62%
Precision 92.0%about 96%
Wait per prompt under 1 msabout 1.5 s

Fast and nearly blind. Rules alone miss five attacks in six. Nearly four times the catches, and precision goes up too. The price is the wait.

Weak spot If the judge model refuses to even look at an attack, the prompt drops back to the weak rules. That happened 81 times in 746.

Security Intelligence RAG

Ask it about any of 358,170 known vulnerabilities and it answers with citations, including which ones are actually being exploited.
