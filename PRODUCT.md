# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: cleared / defense employers hiring for agentic AI, automation, and AI security roles, arriving from a resume, LinkedIn, or GitHub link. Secondary: engineers who follow a link through to the public repos.

## Product Purpose

Jace Griffith's personal portfolio at cyber-griffith.github.io. It has to show what Jace builds, wow the visitor, feel like Jace, and make contacting Jace one click.

## Positioning

Agentic AI & Automation Engineer with an AI security and agent security background (owner's words, 2026-11-17).

## Operating Context

- Static site on GitHub Pages: hand-written HTML, CSS, and JS, no build step, no framework, no third-party requests.
- Public projects live at github.com/cyber-griffith.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **No employer work on the site** (owner decision, 2026-11-17: "I don't want any of my job information that gives away stuff to people and gives them ideas"). No employer workflow, internal tool names, internal figures, employer name, or platform name. The current role appears only as a generic line in the story.
- **No location** anywhere on the site (owner decision, 2026-11-17).
- Public contact is email, GitHub, LinkedIn, and Credly only. No phone number, no resume download.
- Detail level: headlines plus key numbers. No resume-level specifics.
- Skills and capabilities are shown generically (the loadout) and through public projects only.
- Sports and genres in "Off the clock" come from `me.js`, which the owner edits directly.
- No invented personal details: no specific teams, artists, or game titles unless the owner supplies them.
- One dark theme.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Jace Griffith. Handle: cyber-griffith. Profile photo: `profile.jpg`.
- Voice: first person, casual and direct; states weak spots as readily as wins.
- Look (owner pin): gamer-esque, a mix of dark-and-electric and loud-and-colourful, in blues, purples and a neon mix. The reference for the level of creativity is the owner's own "Neon Bowl" project: a living cyberpunk world you explore, not a dashboard.
- It must not look AI-generated: no generic card grids or grid backgrounds as the main event; hand-made pixel art and real interactivity instead.
- Owner said "Off the clock" and both achievements sections are right as they are; change them only by adding to them.

## Evidence on Hand

- Public projects with committed artifacts: Red Team Attack Simulator (`results/attack_log.jsonl`, mirrored in `field-data.js`), Security Intelligence RAG, Prompt Shield.
- Certifications: CAISP, SecAI+, Proofpoint AI Data Security Specialist, Proofpoint AI Agent Security Specialist, AI-102, AI-900, CySA+, Security+, LPI Linux Essentials. Verifiable on Credly.
- Interests the owner supplied: sports (basketball, hockey, football, tennis, skateboarding), music across every genre (metal, country, pop, rap, electronic, dubstep), gaming, concerts, skating.

## Product Principles

1. It has to feel like its owner. Personality is a requirement, not decoration.
2. Wow first: the visitor should want to play with it.
3. Public work only, with the weak spots stated.
4. Short copy. The visitor has a minute.
5. Nothing that gives away employer work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-reachable controls with visible focus, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, readable with JavaScript off, responsive down to 360px. Pronouns for the owner are not stated; copy is first person.
